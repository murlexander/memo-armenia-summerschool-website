# Brief

What the Person in History team asked for, as of 2026-11-10. The team's working copy for the header, home text and footer is the Drive doc «Главная, шапка, подвал и меню»; this file records structure and decisions, not copy.

## Structure (Russian version first)

- **Header:** title plus a short phrase about the project.
- **Menu:** three large buttons, LOST, FOUND and ПОКР АЙРУМ, one per page. If the design allows, together they compose the title.
- **LOST (home):** a field of photographed objects shown as cut-outs. Every object links to exactly one project. A project may have several objects. No object stands alone, because that would make navigation too hard to follow.
- **FOUND:** a catalogue of all projects in any visual form (tiles, circles or photos), each opening the project page. This extra click is deliberate: some visitors, such as donors, want a list rather than the object field.
- **Project page:** Название (title), Команда (team), Из чего сделано (what it is made from), История проекта (project history), От автора (from the author), plus photos and video. The template is the Drive doc «Формат текстов второй страницы».
- **ПОКР АЙРУМ:** a longread written by the curator, expected the week of 2026-11-16, with inline photos and YouTube videos. This replaces the prototype's Research page.
- **Map (optional, lowest priority):** places the projects touched. The current source is a Google My Maps map; drawing a custom map is costly and undecided.
- **Footer:** «Совместный проект летнего лагеря "Человек в истории" и Green Camp»; logos of Человек в истории, Zukunft MEMORIAL and Green Camp; Instagram @personinhistory; Green Camp address to be confirmed.

## Home text variants

1. Title plus a short description.
2. Title plus a prompt to click the objects (the prototype's current approach).
3. Title plus a list of keywords. The keywords may become tags: a third way into the projects, besides objects and the catalogue.

The curator prefers variant 3 and asked to see more than one variant before choosing. Build them switchable for review.

## Working agreement

Alex builds a draft with real texts, the full structure and navigation, then schedules a call with the team to review it.
